Jiffy

Press a shortcut, drag a region, get a GIF. For Linux desktops on X11 (Mint, Ubuntu, Debian). Version @VERSION@.

Install

curl -fsSL https://wh1t3-r0s3.github.io/jiffy/install.sh | bash

Updates arrive with your normal system updates.

Use

  • Press the shortcut (the installer tells you which, usually Shift+Print).
  • Drag a region, click a window, or press Space for the whole screen. Esc cancels.
  • Press the shortcut again (or Stop) to finish. The GIF is saved and copied, so Ctrl+V pastes it.
  • Settings: run jiffy settings or open Jiffy Settings from the menu.
